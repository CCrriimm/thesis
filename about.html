RESTAURANT POLICY

House Rules & Guidelines

To ensure a smooth, enjoyable, and fair dining experience for all guests, please take note of our house guidelines.

No Sharing / Leftovers

Strictly no sharing for unlimited promos. Leftover chicken will be charged at regular rates.

Serving Time

We don't serve fast food, we serve GOOD FOOD fresh! Please give us ~20 mins to prepare your order.

All UNLI or NO UNLI

To avoid sharing issues, all persons per dining table must go UNLI or NO UNLI AT ALL.

Refill Protocol

First serving consists of 4 pcs of wings. NO refills allowed until the initial serving is finished.

2-Hour Time Limit

Enjoy unlimited chicken wings, rice, and sides for a full TWO (2) HOURS.

No Outside Food

Food & drinks brought from outside food establishments are strictly NOT ALLOWED.

Kids Under 3ft FREE

Children under 3 feet dine for FREE when accompanying dining adult customers.

Served With Rice

All unlimited promo packages are AUTOMATICALLY served with steamed white rice.
